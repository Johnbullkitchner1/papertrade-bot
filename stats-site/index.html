How profit and loss work here

Every trade is planned to lose a little. That loss is the price paid for the PAPER it earns.

One trade, two bets

The bot puts $10 on the price going up and $10 on it going down, on the same coin at the same moment, at 100× leverage. One side wins and the other loses, so price direction mostly cancels out.

Where the loss comes from

Fees make each trade end slightly negative, usually about $0.80. Papertrade pays PAPER on the losing side, about 60 PAPER per trade.

How the loss is capped

The bot stops opening trades once spending reaches the budget or PAPER held reaches the target. A trade closes when the price moves 0.55%, or earlier if one side gets close to being force-closed.

What comes back

PAPER is staked and earns a share of Papertrade's fees, paid in USDC. So far that has been a few cents a day, far less than what was spent.
